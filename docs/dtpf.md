# The Dimensionally Transcendent Plasma Forge (DTPF)

After GTNH's: a vast hall that forges plasma into matter. The machine is `gtceu:dtpf` (af9-core `PlasmaForgeMachine`; the
structure and the machine definition in `kubejs/startup_scripts/gtceu/dtpf.js`; the recipes in
`kubejs/server_scripts/mods/gtceu/dtpf.js`). The structure is AF9's own design, not a copy of GTNH's.

## 1. The structure (37 x 29 x 37)

An industrial skeleton, all beams and bracing (no towers, spires or ornament), generated by one function,
`dtpfCell(x, y, z)`, which is the whole design: change it and the aisles follow. About 6,100 blocks. From the ground up:

| Part | Where | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | a square of side 37 at y 0 with two diagonals of coils and two square rings (coil / mk3 casing) inlaid; a plinth for the core | `gtceu:stable_machine_casing` (B), coils (K), mk3 fusion casing (T) |
| Wall | a plain square ring on the edge, 3 high; a gate (5 wide, 2 high) in the middle of each side, the front one closed round the controller; every block of the wall may be a hatch | `gtceu:fusion_casing` (W) |
| Corner pylons | four open lattice towers to y 23: 5 x 5, four 2 x 2 posts with a ring girder every 6 and X bracing in every face; a flat 2-thick slab on top with a coil | B, T, mk2 fusion casing (F), coils |
| Plasma core | a 9 x 9 box, y 2-17, shell of mk2 fusion casing with glass windows and coil rings at y 5, 10, 15; a glass plasma bar up its middle; a flat roof with four exhaust stacks and a hub post | F, `gtceu:fusion_glass` (G), K, T |
| Diagonal struts | from every pylon two struts of 3 blocks' width that cross: one from the pylon's top (y 21) down to the core's foot (y 5), one from its waist (y 9) up to the core's roof (y 20); coil rings along them | F, K |
| Beam rings | two square rings hung between the struts at y 11-13 (side 19 and side 25), and four spokes from the core to the first | T, F |
| Top | box girders (chords at y 21 and 23, a post every second block) joining the pylon tops, and a diagonal cross of beams at y 25 over the core | F, K, T |

The controller is in the front wall, two up (the face is GTNH's DTPF overlay). The preview shows the hall level by level.

## 2. The machine

* **Hatches** only in the wall: up to 4 energy, 8 fluid input, 4 fluid output, 4 item input and 4 item output buses, 1
  maintenance (`setMaxGlobalLimited`: maximums, never required counts).
* **Gate** (`FORGE_GATE`): it starts a recipe only when its hatches can supply the recipe's full EU/t (voltage x amps of all
  of them), as the lithography stations do.
* **Running-time ramp** (`RAMP`, from GTNH's discount): while a recipe runs the forge warms up, 1 tick of warmth per tick of
  running time, to 30 minutes; a pause cools it three times as fast. Warmth scales the recipe's EU/t by up to **-50 %** and
  its run time by up to **-25 %**. A broken structure starts cold. The console and Jade show the ramp.
* Perfect overclocks.

## 3. The recipes (`plasma_forge`, 6 items, 6 items out, 6 fluids, 3 fluids out; UHV voltage, 100 A)

| Recipe | Takes | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| `af9:dtpf/strange_matter_plasma` | 8 strange matter dust, 2,000 mB Endion | 2,000 mB strange matter plasma |
| `af9:dtpf/chromodynium_plasma` | 4 chromodynium dust, 4,000 mB Endion | 2,000 mB chromodynium plasma |
| `af9:dtpf/anti_matter_plasma` | 8 anti-matter dust, 2,000 mB Endion | 2,000 mB Anti Matter Plasma |
| `af9:dtpf/superstate_star_matter_plasma` | 4 superstate star matter dust, 4,000 mB Endion | 2,000 mB Superstate Star Matter Plasma |
| `af9:dtpf/chromodynium_plates` | 2,000 mB chromodynium plasma | 6 chromodynium plates (4 dust: 1.5x) |
| `af9:dtpf/superstate_star_matter_plates` | 2,000 mB superstate star matter plasma | 6 superstate star matter plates (4 dust: 1.5x) |
| `af9:dtpf/tritanium_plates` | 4 tritanium ingots, 500 mB strange matter plasma | 6 tritanium plates |
| `af9:dtpf/neutronium_plates` | 4 neutronium ingots, 1,000 mB strange matter plasma | 6 neutronium plates |

The four plasmas are AF9 Core's fluids `gtceu:strange_matter_plasma`, `gtceu:chromodynium_plasma`,
`gtceu:anti_matter_plasma` and `gtceu:superstate_star_matter_plasma` (`.plasma()` on the
materials). The controller is made in the assembler (`af9:dtpf`, UV).

## 4. AI Acceleration Card

`af9:ai_acceleration_card` (AF9 Core `registry/AF9Items`): TPU and memristor on a wetware board,
traced in 128 fine sanguinite wire. UHV circuit assembler, plasma solder canonical (tin stays as a dead
budget option), clean room — `af9:ai_acceleration_card` in `kubejs/server_scripts/mods/gtceu/circuits_af9.js`.
The compute card of the new DTPF: its recipes take the card.
