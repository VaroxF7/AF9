# The Dimensionally Transcendent Plasma Forge (DTPF)

After GTNH's: a vast hall that forges plasma into matter. The machine is `gtceu:dtpf` (af9-core `PlasmaForgeMachine`; the
structure and the machine definition in `kubejs/startup_scripts/gtceu/dtpf.js`; the recipes in
`kubejs/server_scripts/mods/gtceu/dtpf.js`). The structure is AF9's own design, not a copy of GTNH's.

## 1. The structure (33 x 27 x 33)

Generated by one function, `dtpfCell(x, y, z)`, which is the whole design: change it and the aisles follow. From the
outside in:

| Part | Where | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | a disc of radius 16.8 at y 0, reaching under the towers; inlaid rings of coils (r 8.6-9.5) and torus casing (r 12.6-13.2); a plinth for the column | `gtceu:stable_machine_casing` (B), coils (K), mk3 fusion casing (T) |
| Wall | a ring r 15.3-16.7, 4 blocks high, eight arches (the front one closed round the controller), crenellated on top | `gtceu:fusion_casing` (W): **every block may be a hatch** |
| Towers | four, at (±13, ±13), 3 x 3, up to y 17, then a glass cap and a spire of coil, glass, coil | `gtceu:high_temperature_smelting_casing` (H), coils, glass |
| Torus | major radius 11.5, tube radius 2.4, round y = 12: a shell one block thick; coil bands every 45 degrees, glass panes in the crown and floor every 22.5 | mk3 fusion casing (T), coils (K), `gtceu:fusion_glass` (G) |
| Spokes and pylons | four hollow spokes along the axes from the torus to the column, four solid pylons on the diagonals from the foundation to the torus | mk2 fusion casing (F), stable casing / coils |
| Column | the plasma chamber, 7 across, y 1-25: windows in sectors, coil rings at y 6, 12, 18, 23, a cap and a spire (y 26) | mk2 fusion casing, glass, coils, H |
| Halo | a ring of glass and coils at y 21, r 6-7.2, on four struts | G, K, F |

The controller is in the front wall, three up (the face is GTNH's DTPF overlay). About 4,000 blocks. The preview shows the
hall level by level.

## 2. The machine

* **Hatches** only in the wall: up to 4 energy, 8 fluid input, 4 fluid output, 4 item input and 4 item output buses, 1
  maintenance (`setMaxGlobalLimited`: maximums, never required counts).
* **Gate** (`FORGE_GATE`): it starts a recipe only when its hatches can supply the recipe's full EU/t (voltage x amps of all
  of them), as the lithography stations do.
* **Running-time ramp** (`RAMP`, from GTNH's discount): while a recipe runs the forge warms up, 1 tick of warmth per tick of
  running time, to 30 minutes; a pause cools it three times as fast. Warmth scales the recipe's EU/t by up to **-50 %** and
  its run time by up to **-25 %**. A broken structure starts cold. The console and Jade show the ramp.
* Perfect overclocks.

## 3. The recipes (`plasma_forge`, 6 items, 6 items out, 6 fluids, 3 fluids out; UHV, 2 A)

| Recipe | Takes | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| `af9:dtpf/strange_matter_plasma` | 8 strange matter dust, 2,000 mB Endion | 2,000 mB strange matter plasma |
| `af9:dtpf/chromodynium_plasma` | 4 chromodynium dust, 4,000 mB Endion | 2,000 mB chromodynium plasma |
| `af9:dtpf/chromodynium_plates` | 2,000 mB chromodynium plasma | 6 chromodynium plates (4 dust: 1.5x) |
| `af9:dtpf/tritanium_plates` | 4 tritanium ingots, 500 mB strange matter plasma | 6 tritanium plates |
| `af9:dtpf/neutronium_plates` | 4 neutronium ingots, 1,000 mB strange matter plasma | 6 neutronium plates |

The two plasmas are AF9 Core's fluids `gtceu:strange_matter_plasma` and `gtceu:chromodynium_plasma` (`.plasma()` on the
materials). The controller is made in the assembler (`af9:dtpf`, UV).
