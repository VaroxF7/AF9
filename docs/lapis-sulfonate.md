# Lapis Butene-II-Sulfonate and Annealed Bronze

Janne's request (2026-10-18). Code: `AF9Materials.lapisSulfonate()` / `annealedBronze()`, `lapis_sulfonate.js`, `annealed_bronze.js`.
Janne writes the recipes that use both. Lapis Butene-II-Sulfonate is the pack's counterpart of GT's iron(III) chloride.

## Lapis Butene-II-Sulfonate (all HV, chemical reactor)

| Step | Takes | Makes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. leach | 4 lapis dust, 2000 mB sulfuric acid | 2000 mB lapis leach liquor, 1000 mB H2S, 2 SiO2 dust |
| 2. burn the sulfide | 2000 mB H2S, 3000 mB O2 (circuit 2) | 2000 mB SO2, 2000 mB water |
| 3. catalyst (once) | 4 vanadium dust, 5000 mB O2 | 2 vanadium pentoxide dust |
| 4. contact | 2000 mB SO2, 1000 mB O2, V2O5 (not consumed) | 2000 mB SO3 |
| 5. sulfonation | 1000 mB butene, 1000 mB SO3 | 1000 mB butene sulfonic acid |
| 6. ion exchange | 2000 mB sulfonic acid, 1000 mB leach liquor | 2000 mB Lapis Butene-II-Sulfonate, 1000 mB sulfuric acid |

The sulfuric acid of step 6 feeds step 1 again. Texture: lapis-blue tint as a placeholder until Janne's own liquid texture is in.

## Annealed Bronze

Bronze ingot + 1000 mB nitrogen, EBF at 1000 K (MV): annealed bronze ingot. Plate and foil come from GT's flags.
