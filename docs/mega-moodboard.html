2 · Silicon & circuits — the factory

Quartz to 1 nm, and everything the chips are made of.

DONESemiconductor factory — quartz → 1 nm

MG-Si → polysilicon (Moon Sand + Artemite dopant) → EBF boule melting → blank wafers → Coater Track → lithography 350 nm down to 1 nm (Line / Scanner / Orbital Station) → cutter dies → circuits. Nine substrates, AF9's own chips (RF, APU, MCU, ASIC, e/m/FE-RAM, VPU, TPU + six research families).

docs/semiconductor-factory.md · photolithography.js + litho_process.js · chip_uses.js / circuits_af9.js

DONETranquillite & Aluminium-Tranquillite — Al-Si rewritten

Aluminium and silicon never belonged in one mixer: the MV bond-wire alloy is now aluminium + Tranquillite, a sea-teal lunar silicate (Moon blob vein, weight 50 — the heaviest of the three Moon ores). All 5 MV wire inputs, the mixer, both alloys quests and the docs moved over; zero aluminium_silicon references remain.

AF9Materials (moonSilicon + electronicsMetallurgy) · vein_moon.js · circuits_af9.js

DONELesolite — the EV SMD mineral

Amber lunar mineral beside artemite/tranquillite on the Moon (blob vein, weight 30). Furnace-smeltable, formula-only so GT adds no shortcuts. Reserved for the EV SMD substrates — the SMD recipes taking it are still to come.

docs/lesolite.md · vein_moon.js · void-miner Moon mode + elevator tier 1

DONENichrome-Platinum alloy — new ACM controller frames

Didn't exist in GT or AF9, so it was registered as one (1:1, frame-capable, EBF-melted, HV mixer) and the Advanced Circuit Manufacturer controller now takes 4 frames of it.

AF9Materials · circuits_af9.js · advanced_circuit_manufacturer.js

DONESolders — high-grade, living, plasma (high-grade got a face)

HV–UV run on high-grade solder (was plain white — now a custom satin-silver icon set with brushed streaks and a whisper of bismuth iridescence, tools/textures/solder.py). Wetware grows living solder sterile; UHV solders atom-by-atom with plasma solder in the Hyper-Intensity Laser Engraver.

docs/solders.md · solders.js · hile.js (5×5×5, laser hatch on top)

DONEAdvanced Circuit Manufacturer + Pico circuits

Genesis pack's circuit plant (17×5×5, UHV on 300 A through the laser hatch, computation hatch) making GTNH's Pico line: board → cleansed → CPU → organized circuit → casing → rack. The DTPF controller is built in it too.

DONEStaged Assembly + step detector + quest line

3×3×4 steel-and-glass hall that builds recipes in stages (1–7 layers, ≤5 inputs each) and only ever accepts the running stage's inputs — feed it step by step or gate feeders off the step detector cover (redstone = next step number, 0 when done; screwdriver inverts). Own 6-quest chapter walks build → hand craft → detector → automation.

3 · Power & matter — fission to star-forging

Where the EU comes from, and what the endgame transmutes.

DONEAsteroid fission — uranium only from space

T1–T4 gregified rockets (Aluminised Hydrolox) → Ceres → Asteroid Field brannerite → yellowcake → FX-1 Reactor (5×5×5, EV, supercritical steam + NaK loop) → spent fuel → Pu-239/241 → Fusion Mk1. Old uranium closed everywhere.

docs/asteroid-fission.md · asteroid_fission.js + rockets.js + fusion_reactor.js

DONEFusion power — Mk1 to Mega

GTNH's fusion plasmas in four steps (helium → … → plutonium-241), each a plasma-turbine fuel at GTNH value; star matter burns, anti-matter fuses in the Mega and forges into Idontknowium in the DTPF.

docs/fusion-power.md · fusion_reactor.js

DONEDTPF — Dimensionally Transcendent Plasma Forge, re-gated

37×29×37 industrial skeleton (own design, code-generated) with the running-time ramp (−50% EU/t, −25% time). Controller rebuilt endgame-grade: 16× UHV circuits, UHV hull, 8× mk3 fusion casing, field gens, 4× AI cards, 32× qubit CPUs, 16× eDRAM, 8 stacks of sanguinite wire, plasma solder — in the Advanced Circuit Manufacturer.

docs/dtpf.md · dtpf.js ×2

DONESanguinite — the UHV superconductor

Blood-red lossless UV 4A wire: neutronium + tritanium dusts, H₂, Ares gas, LXA-1 → crude → Sanguinite Hearth Furnace (6,000 K, helium-boosted) → vacuum freezer under supercooled hydrogen. The DTPF's 8 wire stacks.

docs/uhv-superconductor.md · uhv_superconductor.js
