Circuit Foundry — progression redesign: bootstrap every tier, NVM builds the Hyper Dimensional Rocket Drive

The pattern, repeated Steam → LuV: ENTRY (expensive first circuit of the tier, gated by the previous tier's end-milestone, first craftable by hand) → CARRY (that circuit builds the whole tier) → MID-TIER milestone (a new processing line unlocks a cheap reprint of the tier's circuits) → END milestone (unlocks the next tier's entry). Past LuV the pattern ends: costs go exponential (2× → 4× → 8–10× → 16× → 32× previous-tier circuits) all the way to NVM.

Pack goal: the NVM endgame is building the Hyper Dimensional Rocket Drive — it eats the top of this ladder.

1 · Deadlock audit — read this first

Every tier entry was traced input-by-input to the tier that can actually craft it. One hard break found, and fixed.

CLEARThe Silicon Paradox (existed, fixed: T1 rocket flies pre-silicon)

Six links, all verified in-repo at the time:

  1. MV's good_integrated_circuit needs ilc_chip, MV's micro_processor needs cpu/ram_chip — circuits_af9.js:138–161.
  2. ilc/ram/cpu print only on silicon wafers (native substrate 0, N350 @ MV) — photolithography.js:60–62.
  3. Silicon boules need 32× electronic-grade Si + tiny artemite dust, and polysilicon comes only from Moon Sand — boule_melting.js:77–80, fab_chemistry.js:90.
  4. Artemite generates only on Earth's Moon — vein_moon.js:4–6.
  5. The Moon used to need a T1 rocket with HV sensor + af9:mcu_chip in the nose cone — old rockets.js.
  6. mcu_chip is silicon-native (photolithography.js:80), and HV circuits need silicon dies (circuits_af9.js:201–205).

Circle was: silicon → Moon → silicon. Fix, implemented in rockets.js: the T1 rocket flies on LV parts (stainless hull, LV sensor/arms/pistons/motors, LV circuits for guidance and flight computer) out of an expensive MV assembler recipe — nothing silicon, nothing above LV components. Moon first, silicon second, paradox gone.

AS-ISEarth Siemens proposal withdrawn: the earlier idea (MV Earth Siemens route + undoped CZ boule) is superseded — with the T1 flying pre-silicon, Moon keeps bulk polysilicon (8/block) and artemite p-doping uncontested. No quest gating was ever needed; the fix is recipes only.

5 · Pack goal — the Hyper Dimensional Rocket Drive PROPOSED

What it is

The NVM endgame: a buildable drive (block + quest line) that retires the T4 Glacio rocket as the headline goal — instant / cross-dimensional flight once ignited. It must eat the ladder's rarest outputs, or the exponential curve is decoration.

Proposed bill (tunable, not soften-able)

  • 4× NVM mainframe + 2× pico_circuit_rack + chromodynium frame + neutronium plates
  • 1× photonic_package + gravistar + strange matter + plasma solder (bulk mB)
  • Assembly-line research on the T4 rocket + Dyson-class power on (300 A heritage)

Quest sketch — new chapter hyperdrive.snbt

  • 1 · Drive Frame (chromodynium + neutronium shell)
  • 2 · The Core (NVM mainframes + pico racks)
  • 3 · Fuel (star-matter plasma — ties docs/fusion-power.md)
  • 4 · Ignition (Dyson power + research)
  • 5 · Beyond Glacio (first jump — pack ending)

Why NVM fits

NVM's memristor + quantum-dot logic is sold as non-volatile memory that holds state without power — the drive that remembers coordinates through a jump. Story and shopping list agree.
