# The Evaporation Condensation Retaining Well (ECRW)

A shallow round pond that boils dilute fluids down and keeps the condensate: the replacement for Mekanism's
evaporation plant and the evaporation GregTech had a while back. The machine is `gtceu:ecrw` (af9-core
`EvaporationWellMachine`; the rake arm is `EcrwArmRender`; the structure and the machine definition in
`kubejs/startup_scripts/gtceu/ecrw.js`; the recipes in `kubejs/server_scripts/mods/gtceu/ecrw.js`).

## 1. The structure (13 x 4 x 17)

The pond is a circle generated by one function, `ecrwCell(x, y, z)`, which is the whole design, with a small machine
house against its front. From the ground up:

| Part | Where | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | a disc at y 0 | stainless casing (B) |
| Wall | a plain ring on the edge, y 0-2 | watertight casing (R), no hatches |
| Rim | the same ring once more along the wall's top | watertight casing, plain (R) |
| Drive pier | a plus up the middle, y 1-3 | stainless casing (C) |
| Machine house | 7 wide, 4 deep, 3 high, hollow, against the front of the pond | watertight casing or any hatch (H) |

The controller is in the machine house's front wall at its foot. Every block of the machine house may be a hatch (up to
2 energy, 4 fluid input, 2 fluid output, 2 item input, 1 item output, 1 maintenance; maximums, never required counts):
all the buses and hatches go there, so the pond's wall stays a clean circle (the render draws it round).

## 2. The machine

* Plain GregTech behaviour with perfect overclocks; no gates, no ramp.
* **The pond, drawn like a round clarifier** (`EcrwArmRender`, only while the pond is formed, no blocks: the pattern
  leaves the pond hollow):
  * the **water**: a translucent surface on exactly the cells the pattern leaves hollow (r < 6.4, outside the pier),
    tinted by the biome's water colour;
  * a smooth **round wall** and a **round pier** in the casings' own textures (`PondWalls` hides the stepped wall, rim
    and pier blocks of a formed well through Embeddium's block render hook, `EcrwArmRender` draws the round ones). The
    controller and every hatch stay real blocks and stand out of the round wall; the floor stays blocks. Without
    Embeddium the stepped blocks stay and a round coping ring on the rim hides the steps from above instead;
  * a steel **track** on the wall's top for the bridge's wheels;
  * the **scraper bridge** (a lattice girder walkway with handrails, as on a German "Klaerwerk Becken"): a turntable on
    the pier, a drive carriage with two wheels and a motor on the rim. It turns once in about 32 s while a recipe runs
    and runs up and down to a stop;
  * the **skimmer**: an angled blade on three struts at the water line under the bridge, pushing the scum (foam patches
    sliding along the blade) to a fixed **scum trough** at the wall, to the controller's left.

## 3. The recipes (`evaporation`, 2 items / 2 items out, 3 fluids / 3 fluids out)

| Recipe | Takes | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| `af9:ecrw/salt_evaporation` | 1,000 mB salt water (GT's mixer: 2 salt dust + water) | 2 salt dust, 800 mB distilled water |

The controller is made in the assembler (`af9:ecrw`, HV): HV hull, stainless plates, tempered glass, HV
pumps, HV circuits.
