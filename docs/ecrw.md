# The Evaporation Condensation Retaining Well (ECRW)

A shallow round pond that boils dilute fluids down and keeps the condensate: the replacement for Mekanism's
evaporation plant and the evaporation GregTech had a while back. The machine is `gtceu:ecrw` (af9-core
`EvaporationWellMachine`; the rake arm is `EcrwArmRender`; the structure and the machine definition in
`kubejs/startup_scripts/gtceu/ecrw.js`; the recipes in `kubejs/server_scripts/mods/gtceu/ecrw.js`).

## 1. The structure (13 x 4 x 13)

The player's own build, restyled block for block: a circle generated by one function, `ecrwCell(x, y, z)`,
which is the whole design. From the ground up:

| Part | Where | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | a disc at y 0 | stainless casing (B) |
| Wall | a plain ring on the edge, y 1-2 | watertight casing (W, hatches) |
| Rim | the same ring once more along the wall's top, y 3 | watertight casing, plain (R) |
| Drive pier | a plus up the middle, y 1-4 | stainless casing (C) |

The controller is in the front wall at y 1. Every block of the wall may be a hatch (up to 2 energy, 4
fluid input, 2 fluid output, 2 item input, 1 item output, 1 maintenance; maximums, never required counts).

## 2. The machine

* Plain GregTech behaviour with perfect overclocks; no gates, no ramp.
* **The pond's water** (`EcrwArmRender`, drawn only while the pond is formed): a translucent surface on exactly the
  cells the pattern leaves hollow (r < 6.4, outside the pier), tinted by the biome's water colour. The rotating bridge
  render was taken out; the machine gets a whole multiblock model of its own. No blocks: the pattern leaves the pond
  hollow.

## 3. The recipes (`evaporation`, 2 items / 2 items out, 3 fluids / 3 fluids out)

| Recipe | Takes | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| `af9:ecrw/salt_evaporation` | 1,000 mB salt water (GT's mixer: 2 salt dust + water) | 2 salt dust, 800 mB distilled water |

The controller is made in the assembler (`af9:ecrw`, HV): HV hull, stainless plates, tempered glass, HV
pumps, HV circuits.
