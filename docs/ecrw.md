# The Evaporation Condensation Retaining Well (ECRW)

A shallow round pond that boils dilute fluids down and keeps the condensate: the replacement for Mekanism's
evaporation plant and the evaporation GregTech had a while back. The machine is `gtceu:ecrw` (af9-core
`EvaporationWellMachine`; the rake arm is `EcrwArmRender`; the structure and the machine definition in
`kubejs/startup_scripts/gtceu/ecrw.js`; the recipes in `kubejs/server_scripts/mods/gtceu/ecrw.js`).

## 1. The structure (15 x 6 x 15)

A circle generated by one function, `ecrwCell(x, y, z)`, which is the whole design: change it and the aisles
follow. From the ground up:

| Part | Where | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| Floor | a disc at y 0 | solid steel casing (B) |
| Wall | a plain ring on the edge, y 1-3, glass at the waterline (y 2) | solid steel (W, hatches) + `gtceu:tempered_glass` (G) |
| Drive pier | a 3 x 3 column up the middle, y 1-4 | `gtceu:tungstensteel_gearbox` (C) |
| Walkway | a half bridge from the back rim to the pier at y 4, rails at y 5, pier cap | `gtceu:steel_frame` (F) |
| Rim | a ring of frames along the wall's top, y 4 | F |

The controller is in the front wall at y 1. Every steel block of the wall may be a hatch (up to 2 energy, 4
fluid input, 2 fluid output, 2 item input, 1 item output, 1 maintenance; maximums, never required counts).

## 2. The machine

* Plain GregTech behaviour with perfect overclocks; no gates, no ramp.
* **The rake** (`EcrwArmRender`, after the other dynamic renders): a steel truss across the pond on the pier,
  with squeegee blades hanging into the brine. It turns while a recipe runs (about 3 turns a minute) and parks
  where it stopped. No blocks: the pattern leaves the pond hollow and the render draws the arm.

## 3. The recipes (`evaporation`, 2 items / 2 items out, 3 fluids / 3 fluids out)

| Recipe | Takes | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| `af9:ecrw/salt_evaporation` | 1,000 mB salt water (GT's mixer: 2 salt dust + water) | 2 salt dust, 800 mB distilled water |

The controller is made in the assembler (`af9:ecrw`, HV): HV hull, stainless plates, tempered glass, HV
pumps, HV circuits.

## 4. Still open

Chem and bio processing lines on this type (concentrated brines, broth reduction). A liquid surface in the
pond (the render draws the arm only; the pond itself stays hollow).
